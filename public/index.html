Will it work on my phone?

Your phone needs eSIM support and must be carrier-unlocked.

Most iPhones from the XR and XS on, Pixel 4 and newer, and Galaxy S20 and newer support eSIM. See the full check.

Keep your number

Bring your current number when you check out. Do not cancel your old service until the transfer finishes.

Have these ready from your current carrier (usually in their app or by asking support for a “port-out PIN” or “transfer PIN”):

  • The phone number you want to keep
  • Your account number
  • A transfer PIN (4–10 digits)
  • The billing ZIP on that account

We check that you have a PIN at checkout, then we drop it. It is never stored. We will ask for it again when we activate your line.

Questions

What network do these plans use?

These Airvoice Wireless plans run on the AT&T network, with 5G+ where it is available and 4G LTE elsewhere.

Is SimNow the same as Airvoice Wireless?

No. SimNow is an independent seller. We are not Airvoice Wireless and are not affiliated with or endorsed by Airvoice or AT&T. Trademarks belong to their owners.

When do I get my eSIM?

After we confirm your order. Your order page will move from Paid to Activating to Active, and the QR code will show up there once the line is activated.

Are taxes and fees included?

Yes. The price you see is the prepaid amount due today, including taxes and fees.

Is there a contract?

No. These are prepaid plans with no contract. After the term ends, service renews at the then-current rate unless you cancel.

Can I keep my number?

Yes. Choose “Transfer my current phone number” at checkout and keep your old service on until the transfer finishes.
