Does my phone support eSIM?

Choose your phone. Most iPhones from the XR/XS on, Pixel 4 and newer, and Galaxy S20 and newer support eSIM.

Is my phone unlocked? Your phone must be carrier-unlocked to use a new eSIM. iPhone: Settings › General › About, look for "Carrier Lock: No SIM restrictions". Android: ask your current carrier, or try another carrier's SIM.
This list covers common US models and isn't complete. Some phones sold by specific carriers or in other countries are different. If you're unsure, check your phone's settings for "Add eSIM".
